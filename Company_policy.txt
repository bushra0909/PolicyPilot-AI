# Company Policy Document

## 1. Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 paid leave days per year. Employees must inform their manager before taking leave. Leave approval depends on the manager's decision and team requirements.

## 2. Working Hours
Employees work from 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees receive a lunch break from 1 PM to 2 PM.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees can work from home two days per week. Employees must coordinate their remote-work schedule with their manager and remain available during working hours.

## 4. Attendance
Employees are expected to arrive on time and attend scheduled meetings. If an employee expects to be late or absent, they should inform their manager as soon as possible.

## 5. Workplace Conduct
Employees must communicate respectfully with colleagues and managers. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are not acceptable in the workplace.

## 6. Data Security
Employees must protect confidential company information. Company passwords must not be shared with other people. Suspicious emails or possible data breaches should be reported to the IT team.

## 7. Equipment
Company equipment should be used responsibly and primarily for authorized work. Employees should report damaged or malfunctioning equipment to the appropriate team.

## 8. Expenses
Employees must obtain the required approval before making purchases on behalf of the company. Expense claims should include receipts and a clear description of the business purpose.
